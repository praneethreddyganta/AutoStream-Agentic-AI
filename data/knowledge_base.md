# AutoStream Knowledge Base

## Pricing Plans & Features

### Basic Plan
- **Price:** $29/month
- **Features:**
  - 10 videos/month
  - 720p resolution rendering

### Pro Plan
- **Price:** $79/month
- **Features:**
  - Unlimited videos/month
  - 4K resolution rendering
